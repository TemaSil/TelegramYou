# Quieter channels, calmer avatars, a Pixel-style lock
- Channels you only read have Mute and Unmute where the message field was
- Channels you're not in have a Join button
- People without a photo get calm Material colours, no more gradients
- The app lock has a Pixel-style keypad that reacts to your touch
- Turning the lock off or changing the PIN now asks for the PIN first
