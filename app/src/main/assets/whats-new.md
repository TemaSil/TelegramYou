# The heart counts down, and captions step aside
- The heart and the @ now mark what you've seen, so the count goes down
- Each tap goes to the next one, and other apps see them as read too
- Videos and photos with comments or reactions keep their bubble again
- In the viewer a long caption folds to three lines; tap it to read on a dimmed photo
- Tap the photo to hide everything over it; the heart and search bar glide in and out
